# VisionFlowAI

VisionFlowAI is an AI-powered traffic camera analysis platform designed to help cities and traffic operators understand road conditions in real time and make smarter traffic-management decisions.

## What it does

- Analyzes live or recorded traffic-camera video
- Detects and tracks vehicles across lanes and intersections
- Estimates traffic density, flow, and congestion
- Flags incidents such as stopped vehicles, collisions, and unusual activity
- Presents actionable insights for traffic-control teams

## How it works

1. Traffic cameras provide video streams.
2. The computer-vision pipeline identifies and tracks road users.
3. Analytics turn detections into traffic metrics and alerts.
4. A dashboard helps operators monitor conditions and respond quickly.

## Core capabilities

| Area | Capability |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle intelligence | Vehicle detection, classification, and tracking |
| Traffic analytics | Counts, speed estimates, queue length, and congestion trends |
| Incident awareness | Alerts for anomalous or potentially unsafe road conditions |
| Decision support | Visual insights to support signal timing and traffic operations |

## Project goals

- Improve traffic flow and reduce congestion
- Support quicker detection and response to incidents
- Provide data-driven insights for smarter city infrastructure
- Build a scalable foundation for multi-camera traffic monitoring
